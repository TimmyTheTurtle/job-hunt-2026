Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the Software Engineer, Simulations position at SpaceX. The opportunity to contribute to the simulation infrastructure supporting Falcon, Dragon, Starlink, and Starship is deeply compelling to me. I have long been motivated by the belief that engineering exists to expand humanity’s capabilities, and the work SpaceX is doing to enable human life beyond Earth represents one of the most meaningful technical challenges of our time.

My background is in software engineering across systems that required navigating complex architectures and integrating new functionality into existing platforms. In my most recent role, I implemented custom functionality in a large enterprise C# and SQL system with numerous undocumented components and legacy integrations. Much of the work involved reverse engineering system behavior and constructing reliable solutions in environments where documentation and clear abstractions were limited. This experience strengthened my ability to reason about system state, trace interactions across layers of software, and deliver production-ready solutions under uncertainty.

A significant portion of my work involved developing queries and logic against a highly transactional ledger-style database where reporting abstractions did not exist. Building reliable functionality required reconstructing derived state directly from atomic records and ensuring correctness across multiple system boundaries. That work reinforced the importance of disciplined reasoning about data flow, correctness, and edge cases—skills that translate naturally into simulation and systems software development.

Alongside my professional experience, I am actively deepening my focus on modern C++ and simulation-oriented systems. I am particularly interested in the intersection of numerical modeling, software architecture, and high-performance computing. The problems involved in building reliable, high-fidelity simulation infrastructure—especially in real-time environments supporting flight systems—are exactly the type of engineering challenges I find most motivating.

What attracts me most to this role is the combination of technical depth and real-world impact. Developing simulation infrastructure that enables engineers to design, test, and validate spacecraft systems requires both strong software engineering fundamentals and the ability to reason about complex physical systems. I am excited by the opportunity to contribute to that work while continuing to grow as a C++ systems engineer within a team operating at the highest level of engineering rigor.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my systems experience, problem-solving approach, and growing specialization in C++ and simulation software could contribute to the continued success of the SpaceX simulations team.

Dorian Klingenberg
