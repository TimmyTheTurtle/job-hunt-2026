## Role summary

ITU is building internal AI readiness and operational transformation across business functions. The consultant supports internal AI strategy and implementation, hands-on agent and copilot deployment, workflow automation, responsible-AI governance, staff enablement, and delivery coordination.

The official terms of reference call for work such as:

- identifying and prioritizing AI and automation opportunities;
- translating business needs into implementable solutions and reusable patterns;
- designing, configuring, testing, and deploying internal AI agents and copilots;
- supporting enterprise search, knowledge retrieval, report drafting, meeting support, and workflow acceleration;
- building automation pipelines and low-code/no-code operational solutions;
- contributing to AI governance, cybersecurity, privacy, and data-governance guardrails; and
- delivering workshops, guidance, templates, adoption support, and transition recommendations.

Required experience includes at least eight years of progressively responsible enterprise digital transformation, AI implementation, automation, or innovation delivery; hands-on enterprise AI or agentic-AI implementation; workflow automation; enterprise platforms, copilots, APIs, and orchestration; and the ability to combine strategic advice with technical delivery.
