# Interview Prep

## Company Research

- Mission: build software used to design, simulate, and manufacture complex products across multiple industries
- Product context from posting: CAD, 3D modeling, simulation, and HPC-oriented infrastructure
- Team context from posting: large-scale server/client architecture supporting demanding workflows and workloads
- Recent news: not captured in repo yet; check before any interview

## Role-Specific Prep

- Technical skills to highlight:
  - systems thinking
  - debugging across boundaries
  - practical delivery ownership
  - maintainability and architecture awareness
  - modern C++ learning trajectory
  - performance-aware engineering mindset
- Projects and examples to discuss:
  - business software delivered under real constraints
  - configuration/workflow systems with ambiguous requirements
  - examples of tracing cross-system behavior and fixing hard bugs
  - recent modern C++ / simulation-oriented learning work

## Questions To Ask Them

1. What does the day-to-day split look like between new feature development, debugging, and performance work?
2. Where are the biggest current bottlenecks: throughput, latency, workflow orchestration, or cross-platform behavior?
3. How much of the role is core C++ server/client work versus Python or Tcl integration?
4. What operating systems does the team actively develop and test on each week?
5. What does success look like for the first six months in this role?

## STAR Stories Ready

1. Example of turning ambiguous requirements into a reliable implementation
2. Example of tracing a bug across system or integration boundaries
3. Example of owning delivery in a small-team or low-scaffolding environment
4. Example of learning a technical domain quickly and becoming productive
