## Role summary

Omada’s agentic systems team supports AI transformation across Engineering, Product, and Design. The role builds AI-powered developer workflows, inference and tool-calling pathways using Claude/AWS Bedrock/LiteLLM/MCP-style gateways, Slack-based ChatOps, AI usage and cost instrumentation, and secure workflows for PHI and other sensitive data.

## Requirements captured

- 8+ years as a software engineer working on infrastructure, developer tools, or enabling internal systems.
- Strong backend services and integration experience.
- Hands-on experience with an LLM platform or orchestration layer such as Bedrock, OpenAI, Anthropic, LiteLLM, or LangChain.
- Modern backend/tooling language such as Ruby, Python, TypeScript/Node, or Go.
- Internal or third-party API integrations into bots, CLIs, ChatOps, or internal tools.
- AWS infrastructure and secure-by-default handling of secrets and sensitive data.
- Cross-functional workflow translation, communication, and documentation.
- Bonus: Slack apps, MCP/tool gateways, observability for AI usage/cost, Terraform, healthcare or regulated-industry experience.
