## Role summary

PointClickCare seeks an engineer to bring AI-powered automation into internal corporate-platform workflows. The team works with LLM-driven agents and intelligent workflows, primarily on Microsoft Azure, to streamline operations and delivery. The role connects IT and operations through practical implementation, integration, testing, evaluation, monitoring, guardrails, and responsible-AI practices.

## Responsibilities captured

- Build and support AI agents and intelligent workflows using Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Foundry, and Copilot Studio.
- Implement AI-powered orchestration and automation for configuration, onboarding, and data-migration workflows.
- Work with integration, implementation, product, and support teams to deliver scalable solutions.
- Develop APIs, scripts, and tools that connect LLM-based agents to enterprise systems, including through Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- Support testing, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, guardrails, and responsible-AI safeguards.
- Contribute to an internal AI delivery model and keep current with major AI platforms.

## Requirements captured

- Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, information systems, or equivalent practical experience.
- 3-5 years of software or cloud engineering and SaaS or enterprise experience.
- Experience building AI-powered workflows using Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio, and/or Now Assist.
- Exposure to AI-agent orchestration or multi-agent systems.
- Hands-on Azure experience, including Azure AI, Functions, Logic Apps, and Cognitive Services.
- Familiarity with RAG or vector-database integration and multiple LLMs.
- Proficiency in Python, C#, or JavaScript/TypeScript.
- Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, and cloud deployment; HL7/FHIR is a plus.
