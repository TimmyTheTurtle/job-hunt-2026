## Role and operating model

Provectus describes itself as a Premier AWS partner and Anthropic Strategic Partner delivering applied AI systems, managed services, and advisory work. The role is embedded customer delivery in Financial Services & Insurance and Healthcare & Life Sciences. Engineers work inside the customer's operation, learn the work from the operator's seat, redesign the function, and ship the system themselves.

The posting's central idea is that the engineer does the customer's job before automating it. The engagement model is:

- embedded in the customer's team and process rather than operating as an outside vendor;
- responsible for real operating problems rather than a narrowly fenced deliverable; and
- autonomous, with ownership of the method and technical outcome.

Provectus says engineers are measured against the business unit's outcome, not merely hours or scope delivered. Work starts from existing industry blueprints and feeds field learning back into those blueprints.

## Required capabilities

- 8+ years building software, with substantial accountable production coding.
- Willingness to spend two to four weeks performing an operator's job before writing the automation.
- Ability to become fluent in an unfamiliar business function quickly and challenge assumptions with domain practitioners.
- Shipped production GenAI/LLM systems beyond demos and notebooks.
- Built or owned an evaluation suite for a nondeterministic system and can explain the measurements.
- Strong engineering fundamentals and productivity in unfamiliar codebases or languages.
- Python and/or TypeScript proficiency.
- AWS cloud-native delivery, including containers, Kubernetes/ECS, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, and operational ownership.
- Credibility with business-unit leaders, CTOs, customer engineers, operators, and executives.
- Comfort with ambiguity, autonomous ownership, and model failure modes.
- Hands-on production experience with Claude Code/Cowork.
- Fluent written and spoken English.

## Preferred background

- Founder, CTO, or engineering-leader experience with a return to individual contribution.
- Depth in financial services, insurance, healthcare, or asset management.
- Consulting, professional services, or other embedded customer-facing delivery.
- Data lakes, warehouses, streaming/real-time analytics, data mesh, data contracts, governance, and data quality.
- MLOps and classical ML, including PyTorch, SageMaker, or MLflow.
- Fine-tuning, distillation, inference, or serving optimization.
- Graph databases such as Neo4j or AWS Neptune.

- Open-source contributions or public writing on applied AI.

## Work expected

### Take the seat

- Perform the operator's work and learn the function from inside.
- Reach working fluency in domains such as insurance underwriting, healthcare revenue cycle, or asset flow.
- Redesign the function with the operator and Forward Deployed Executive; discovery, user research, and PRD work are folded into that redesign.

### Build

- Ship production GenAI systems into customer environments, including LLM applications, agentic workflows, retrieval and structured-extraction pipelines, and supporting services.
- Build the evaluation harness before the feature and tie quality measures to the business KPI.
- Write backend, data-pipeline, and AI-layer code, primarily in Python and TypeScript.
- Deploy containerized, observable, maintainable systems on AWS or the customer's required cloud.
- Improve the shared blueprint from what is learned in each engagement.

### Own the outcome

- Pair with a Forward Deployed Executive who carries the business-unit KPI.
- Drive adoption and treat a workflow that users route around as not shipped.
- Communicate credibly with customer engineers, operators, and executives, including disagreement when needed.
- Shape commitments and scope before committing to the build.

## What Provectus offers

- Delivery work across Cowork Activation, Agentic SDLC, and Blueprint Activations in Financial Services and Healthcare.
- A chance to shape enterprise AI adoption from strategy through first deployment.
- Small, senior forward-deployed teams alongside Principal Architects and Forward Deployed Engineers.
- A growing delivery practice where engineers help build tooling and frameworks.
- Remote-friendly culture.

## Hiring process

1. Introductory conversation about the role, background, and desired work.
2. Two live engineering sessions in the candidate's own editor; an LLM assistant may be used, but autocomplete/agentic coding tools are not.
3. A redesign session using an unfamiliar business function; no LLMs are used for this session.
4. Team and practice conversation.
