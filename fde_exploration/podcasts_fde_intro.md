# Podcasts For FDE Intro

Date: 2026-07-23

## Purpose

This is a curated list of podcasts and episodes that help explain the work around
forward-deployed AI engineering.

The best podcast sources right now are less about the formal title and more about:

- shipping AI products
- production agent workflows
- evaluation, governance, and observability
- the shift from coding alone to systems and workflow ownership

## Recommended Listening Order

1. role and career framing
2. shipping AI products
3. production agent workflows
4. infrastructure, governance, and system reliability

## Recommended Podcasts And Episodes

### 1. Beyond Coding Podcast

Link:
- [Beyond Coding Podcast playlist](https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLX6ivsCcfHDOzMIUioOofyF-h-niZL6Qj)

- `AI Won't Replace Software Engineers, But This Might (CEO Perspective)` - December 9, 2025

## Best "Start Here" Picks

If you only listen to three things first:

1. `Forward Deployed Engineer: The Role Up 800%`
2. `AI Engineering: Skill Stack, Agents, LLMOps, and How to Ship AI Products`
3. `AI Agents: The Models Are Ready, The Systems Aren't`

Why:
- together they cover role framing, shipping discipline, and production systems thinking

## How To Use These Podcasts

Best pattern:

- use `Beyond Coding` for role orientation
- use `DataTalks.Club` and `ODSC` for shipping discipline
- use the others as background while walking, driving, or doing low-cognitive work

These are strongest when they sharpen project choices, not when they become passive consumption.
