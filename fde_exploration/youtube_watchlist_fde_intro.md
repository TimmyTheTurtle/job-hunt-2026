# YouTube Watchlist For FDE Intro

Date: 2026-07-23

## Purpose

This is a curated watchlist for getting oriented to forward-deployed AI engineering.

It is not a list of perfect canonical lectures on the exact title.
The better pattern on YouTube right now is a mix of:

- FDE role discussions
- production AI workflow discussions
- enterprise AI implementation conversations
- monitoring, governance, and human-in-loop discussions

The goal is to understand:

- what the role is
- why it is growing
- what the actual work looks like
- how production AI workflow thinking differs from demo thinking

## Recommended Watch Order

1. role definition
2. market context
3. production AI reality
4. workflow operations
5. enterprise implementation and governance

## Suggested Note-Taking Questions

Use these while watching:

- What part of the role is discovery, implementation, operations, and handoff?
- What parts of the work seem closest to my existing strengths?
- What parts expose the biggest skill gaps?
- What specific project ideas become more obvious after this?
- What vocabulary shows up repeatedly that should influence resume or LinkedIn framing?
- What failure modes come up more than once?

## What This Watchlist Is Best For

This watchlist is best for:

- fast orientation to the FDE idea
- understanding the role from the implementation side
- seeing how enterprise AI work is discussed by practitioners
- identifying vocabulary and expectations for future applications

## What It Will Not Fully Teach

This watchlist will not replace:

- hands-on project work
- API/auth/integration practice
- observability and ops work
- human-review product design
- customer workflow scoping practice

Treat it as orientation and vocabulary-building, not as the whole curriculum.
