# FDE Summary Options

Date: 2026-07-23

Use these as tested variants for resume headlines, top summaries, LinkedIn About blurbs, and
application forms.

## Headlines

1. Forward Deployed Engineer
2. AI Solutions Engineer
3. Forward Deployed AI Engineer
4. Implementation Engineer, AI Systems
5. Systems-Minded Software Engineer for AI Workflows

## Summary Option A - Default FDE

Forward Deployed Engineer with a C#/.NET software engineering background and hands-on work in
workflow systems, integration-heavy environments, and applied AI prototypes. Strong at entering
messy technical situations, translating ambiguous requirements into structured solutions, and
debugging across product, data, and deployment boundaries. Interested in implementation-heavy AI
roles where customer workflows, human review, and real operational constraints matter.

## Summary Option B - AI Solutions Engineer

AI Solutions Engineer with experience in enterprise software, workflow automation, deployment,
and domain-constrained business systems. Builds practical AI-enabled workflows using structured
artifacts, deterministic checks, and human-reviewable outputs rather than black-box automation.
Best fit for roles that combine coding, integration, technical discovery, and customer-facing
problem solving.

## Summary Option C - Bridge To Applied AI

Systems-minded software engineer moving into forward-deployed and implementation-heavy AI work.
Background includes enterprise C# systems, CPQ and business workflow software, compliance-related
automation, and AI-assisted document-analysis prototypes. Strong fit for roles that require
ownership, debugging, integration judgment, and usable outcomes inside messy real environments.
