# FDE Positioning

Date: 2026-07-23

## Why This Pivot Exists

Forward Deployed Engineering looks like a good first island because it is often easier to enter
than a pure platform-AI role while paying better than generic software positions.

The market usually asks less often for "already shipped frontier AI platforms at scale" and more
often for:

- strong engineering fundamentals,
- comfort with ambiguity,
- customer-facing technical judgment,
- integration and deployment skill,
- workflow design,
- debugging under real constraints,
- and the ability to turn a messy business need into a working system.

That aligns well with the strongest repo evidence.

## The Reframed Story

Primary FDE story:

> I am a systems-minded software engineer who does well in messy environments. I translate vague
> requirements into structured workflows, integrate tools and systems, debug blockers across
> boundaries, and ship usable results. My current AI work makes me especially interested in
> forward-deployed and implementation-heavy roles where AI has to work inside real business
> processes.

This is a different entry point from the main repo identity:

> Applied AI Systems Engineer building document intelligence and evidence-oriented workflows.

That deeper identity remains true. It just stops being the first thing the employer has to buy.

## What To Lead With

Lead with:

- Finys: undocumented systems, transactional data, derived-state reconstruction, production correctness
- RenoNerd / WindowConfigurator: domain workflows, quoting/configuration logic, business constraints, deployment reality
- Caribou: integrations, client environments, remote troubleshooting, deployment testing
- legal-tech-debt: proof that AI workflow implementation is real, structured, and bounded
- Areva: compliance-sensitive automation with measurable time savings

Use SED as supporting depth, not the headline.

## What The FDE Sell Is Not

Do not sell:

- "I am a deep AI researcher"
- "I already own a mature production LLM platform"
- "I am primarily a thought leader writing about AI systems"
- "I want to be near customers only as a stepping stone until a better role appears"

The FDE pitch only works if the implementation/operator side sounds genuine.

## Safe Target Titles

- Forward Deployed Engineer
- Forward Deployed AI Engineer
- AI Solutions Engineer
- Implementation Engineer, AI
- Technical Consultant, AI Platform
- Solutions Architect when code ownership is real
- Applied AI Implementation Engineer

## Unsafe Or Risky Titles

- Sales Engineer, if the build component is weak
- Solutions Consultant, if mostly presentations and scoping
- Customer Success Engineer, if success metrics dominate over shipping
- Developer Advocate, unless explicitly wanted
- AI Strategist / Transformation Consultant with little coding

## Interview Positioning Angle

When asked "why FDE?":

> The part of engineering I am consistently strongest at is making systems work in reality.
> I like technical ambiguity when there is a concrete outcome on the other side of it. The
> interesting challenge is not only model behavior; it is shaping software, workflow, data,
> and human review into something a customer can actually use. That is why forward-deployed or
> implementation-heavy AI roles make sense for me right now.
