# Books For FDE Foundations

Date: 2026-07-23

## Purpose

This is a curated book list for building the technical and operational foundations behind
forward-deployed AI engineering.

These are not "FDE title" books.
They are the books most likely to help with the actual work:

- choosing the right AI workflow
- moving from PoC to production
- building retrieval and agent systems with boundaries
- understanding governance, observability, and delivery trade-offs

## Recommended Reading Order

1. practical production AI systems
2. retrieval and workflow architecture
3. provider/platform-specific production patterns
4. deeper framework-specific implementation

## Recommended Books

### 1. Hands-On RAG for Production

Link:
- [Hands-On RAG for Production](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-rag-for/9798341621701/)

Surfaced by search as:
- published May 2026

Why read:
- strongest direct match to your document, retrieval, evidence, and production workflow interests
- explicitly production-oriented rather than "chat with PDF" oriented
- covers ingestion flow, query flow, explainability, access controls, and security

Maps to learning path:
- enterprise integration delivery
- production operations for AI workflows
- reviewable AI product design
- security and governance

Best for:
- legal-tech-debt
- article research console
- any evidence-oriented FDE workflow

### 2. Generative AI at AWS

Link:
- [Generative AI at AWS](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/generative-ai-at/9781806104451/)

Surfaced by search as:
- current O'Reilly catalog title

Why read:
- very strong bridge between business goals and technical delivery
- emphasizes MVP-to-production thinking, governance, scaling, and responsible AI
- useful even if you do not become AWS-specific

Maps to learning path:
- workflow discovery and solution scoping
- business value framing
- security and governance
- handoff and operational delivery

Best for:
- learning how enterprise AI programs are framed
- understanding "what production-grade means" in a customer environment

### 3. Agentic AI with Microsoft Foundry

Link:
- [Agentic AI with Microsoft Foundry](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/agentic-ai-with/9781806673957/)

Surfaced by search as:
- published April 2026

Why read:
- useful if you want a structured platform-centered view of production agent systems
- includes tools, grounding, evaluation, governance, and responsible AI
- likely closer than some Python-first resources to enterprise buyers and Microsoft-heavy shops

Maps to learning path:
- production operations
- workflow orchestration
- auth and platform boundary thinking
- governance and evaluation

Best for:
- enterprise AI context
- internal platform and implementation roles

### 4. Generative AI with LangChain, Second Edition

Link:
- [Generative AI with LangChain - Second Edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/generative-ai-with/9781837022014/)

Surfaced by search as:
- current O'Reilly catalog title

Why read:
- good for implementation detail around RAG, LangGraph, multi-agent patterns, testing, and monitoring
- more framework-shaped than the broader books above

Maps to learning path:
- workflow orchestration
- reviewable AI system design
- observability and testing

Best for:
- building concrete learning projects
- getting from concept to implementation faster

### 5. How Large Language Models Work

Link:
- [How Large Language Models Work](https://livebook.manning.com/book/how-large-language-models-work/welcome)

Surfaced by search as:
- current Manning liveBook listing

Why read:
- not an FDE delivery manual
- useful for strengthening fundamentals so you can reason better about system trade-offs
- helps keep you from cargo-culting agent patterns

Maps to learning path:
- model/tool/workflow judgment
- choosing where AI belongs and where it does not

Best for:
- conceptual clarity
- grounding the more practical books

## Best "Start Here" Picks

If you only pick two:

1. `Hands-On RAG for Production`
2. `Generative AI at AWS`

Why:
- together they cover both your strongest natural direction and the broader production-delivery
  mindset behind FDE work

## What These Books Will Not Replace

These books will not replace:

- hands-on auth and API work
- workflow review UI practice
- customer discovery practice
- deployment and ops experience

Use them to sharpen project choices and implementation discipline, not as a substitute for building.
