# Series 3 — Product Discovery in the AI Age

**Audience:** Product engineers, technical founders, engineering leads tired of the MVP treadmill.
**Arc:** Reframe product discovery as a scientific experiment program, not a single-bet pivot game.
**Platform:** LinkedIn and personal site. Cross-links to Series 1 and 2.

This series is distinct from both existing series:
- Series 1 diagnoses the vibe coding problem in day-to-day development
- Series 2 addresses the engineering discipline for non-deterministic systems
- Series 3 addresses product discovery methodology — what happens before you build the real thing

The central argument: AI has broken the economics of prototyping so thoroughly that the
traditional MVP model — one bet, observe, pivot or persevere — is no longer the optimal
strategy. When experiments are cheap, you run many. When you run many and preserve the
history, the product reveals itself. The path of least resistance is derived, not chosen.
