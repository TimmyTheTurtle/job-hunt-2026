# Series 3 — Product Discovery in the AI Age

**Audience:** Product engineers, technical founders, engineering leads tired of the MVP treadmill.
**Arc:** The healthy version of exploration: treat product discovery as a disciplined experiment
program with preserved evidence, explicit learning, and a clear exit into requirements.
**Platform:** LinkedIn and personal site. Cross-links to Series 1 and 2.

This series is distinct from both existing series:
- Series 1 diagnoses what happens when exploratory AI-assisted development never really ends
- Series 2 addresses the engineering discipline for non-deterministic systems
- Series 3 addresses product discovery methodology — how to explore broadly without getting
  trapped in endless prototype mode

The central argument: AI has broken the economics of prototyping so thoroughly that the
traditional MVP model — one bet, observe, pivot or persevere — is no longer the optimal
strategy. When experiments are cheap, you run many. When you run many, preserve the evidence,
and make the learning legible, the product reveals itself. The path of least resistance is
derived, not chosen. Series 3 is the positive mirror of Series 1: exploratory work with an
explicit memory and an exit condition.
