# S5-A3 — LLMs, Solvers, and the New Reasoning Stack

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 3 of 7

---

## Thesis

The most practically important neuro-symbolic pattern in 2026 may be the hybrid reasoning stack:
LLMs for language, decomposition, and heuristics; symbolic solvers for consistency, search,
formal reasoning, and bounded correctness.

---

## Key Claims

- Hybrid reasoning systems may currently be more promising than monolithic "reasoning models"
- Solver-backed systems help where exactness matters
- LLMs are useful interfaces to symbolic machinery, not replacements for it
- This is a systems design argument as much as a model argument
