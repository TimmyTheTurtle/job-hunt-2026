# S5-A4 — Knowledge Graphs, Typed Memory, and Explicit World Models

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 4 of 7

---

## Thesis

One of the strongest neuro-symbolic currents today is the return of explicit world structure:
knowledge graphs, typed memory, ontologies, and relation-aware retrieval. These systems matter
because some reasoning problems are fundamentally about relations, constraints, and absence,
not just semantic similarity.

---

## Key Claims

- Graphs and typed memory are among the most practical symbolic layers in modern AI stacks
- They help with inspectability, multi-hop reasoning, and gap detection
- This is especially relevant to legal/compliance and other document-heavy domains
- World models do not have to be hand-built from scratch to be useful
