# S5-A7 — Where I Think Neuro-Symbolic AI Actually Lands

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 7 of 7 — the synthesis

---

## Thesis

Neuro-symbolic AI is probably not one thing and not one future. Its real value is likely to be
uneven, domain-specific, and architectural: strongest where explicit structure, typed memory,
constraints, and formal interfaces solve concrete problems that raw neural systems continue to
handle badly.

---

## Key Claims

- The field is real, but overclaimed in places
- Hybrids and symbolic scaffolding may matter more than deep theoretical purity
- Legal/compliance, graph reasoning, constrained workflows, and control surfaces look especially
  promising
- The author's own work naturally intersects with this through typed obligations, graph-backed
  reasoning, and bounded language-mediated systems

---

## Main Points to Discuss

- Where the strongest evidence currently is
- Where the field is still muddy or marketing-heavy
- How this connects to Legal Tech Debt and related system design work
- What to watch over the next 12-24 months
