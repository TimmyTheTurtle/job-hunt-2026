# S5-A1 — Why Neuro-Symbolic AI Is Back

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 1 of 7 — the opener

---

## Thesis

Neuro-symbolic AI is back because the current AI stack has become strong enough to be useful and
weak enough to be dangerous. Once black-box systems are asked to reason, plan, verify, or act
under real constraints, symbolic structure starts reappearing as a practical necessity.

---

## Key Claims

- The revival is not nostalgia for GOFAI
- The pressure is coming from reasoning, trust, and control problems
- The strongest 2026 question is not "neural or symbolic?" but "where is explicit structure
  earning its cost?"
- The field's center of gravity has shifted toward hybrids around LLMs, retrieval, graphs,
  solvers, and typed constraints

---

## Main Points to Discuss

- What changed between the old neuro-symbolic promise and the 2025-2026 moment
- Why black-box model scale revived interest in explicit structure
- Why this is especially relevant in legal/compliance and language-mediated control systems
- Distinguish "making models better at reasoning" from "building systems with better reasoning"
- Tease the taxonomy cleanup in A2
