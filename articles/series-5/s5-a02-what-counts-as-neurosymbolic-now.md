# S5-A2 — What Counts as Neuro-Symbolic AI Now?

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 2 of 7

---

## Thesis

"Neuro-symbolic AI" is being used too loosely. Not every structured workflow, retrieval layer,
or tool-using agent is genuinely neuro-symbolic. The field needs a cleaner taxonomy if the term
is going to remain useful.

---

## Key Claims

- Taxonomy confusion is now one of the biggest barriers to useful discussion
- There is a meaningful difference between deep integration and modular hybridization
- Some systems are genuinely neuro-symbolic; some are better described as symbolic scaffolding
  around black-box models
- The distinction matters for claims about reasoning, verification, and interpretability

---

## Main Points to Discuss

- Deeply integrated neural-symbolic systems vs hybrid LLM-plus-symbolic systems
- Retrieval, graphs, and planners: where they fit and where they do not
- Program synthesis, DSLs, and constraint systems as adjacent categories
- Why naming the category correctly matters for research and practice
- Tease reasoning hybrids in A3
