# S5-A5 — Programs, Constraints, and Control Surfaces

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 5 of 7

---

## Thesis

Another live branch of neuro-symbolic AI is the return of program-like structure: program
synthesis, typed DSLs, constraint systems, formal interfaces, and planner-backed control. The
important question is not whether these feel symbolic. It is whether they create better failure
boundaries and more controllable systems.

---

## Key Claims

- Program-like representations are a major symbolic re-entry point in current AI
- Constraints and DSLs are often more useful than vague "reasoning" claims
- The control surface matters as much as the model
- This is highly relevant to speech interfaces, tool use, and workflow automation
