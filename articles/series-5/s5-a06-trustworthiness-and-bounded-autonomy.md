# S5-A6 — Trustworthiness, Verification, and Bounded Autonomy

**Status:** Skeleton
**Series position:** 6 of 7

---

## Thesis

The most convincing argument for neuro-symbolic AI may not be "better intelligence." It may be
better boundedness. In high-stakes systems, symbolic structure becomes attractive because it can
improve verification, traceability, inspectability, and controlled failure behavior.

---

## Key Claims

- Trustworthiness is one of the strongest present-day arguments for neuro-symbolic methods
- Symbolic layers help when failure boundaries must be legible
- This matters most in regulated, safety-sensitive, and workflow-critical systems
- Neuro-symbolic AI should be judged partly by operational behavior, not only benchmark scores
