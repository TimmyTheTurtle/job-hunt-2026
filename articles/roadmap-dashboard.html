Gantt - 26 weeks

curriculum gate draft in parallel read first build critical path build long game S1 release cadence blocked to unlocking

Milestones: week 1 starts curriculum and drafting, week 4 aims for demo and site, week 6 opens the release gate, week 7 starts article publication.

Curriculum C1-C8

This is the strongest Pluralsight-backed Applied AI engineering coverage track. It is the explicit publication gate for the article series.

Series 1 - The Vibe Coding Problem

Draft in parallel during the curriculum. Release begins after week 6, with S1-A1 first.

Series 1 still leads the public narrative, but now from a stronger base. The curriculum is there to make the technical prescriptions more grounded before these pieces go live.

Series 2 - AI Systems Engineering

This series benefits the most from the curriculum gate because it is where shared AI-engineering patterns and standards matter most.

Use the six-week curriculum to sharpen the claims here. That is especially true for workflow state, retrieval, MCP, observability, and eval-related articles.

Series 3 - Product Discovery in the AI Age

These can still be drafted immediately. They are less blocked by tooling than Series 2, but publication still waits for the gate.

Series 4 - Legal Tech Debt

The curriculum does not replace legal-tech-debt research, but it improves the AI-systems framing around extraction, retrieval, telemetry, and eval discipline.

Build Items B1-B10

These continue in parallel and after the curriculum. They unlock the blocked articles and the stronger demo-backed end of the roadmap.
