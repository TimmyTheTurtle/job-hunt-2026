# RECRUITER RESPONSE RULES FOR CODEX

## Objective

Generate recruiter responses that are polite, credible, concise, and easy to reply to.

## Tone

Responses should be:
- professional
- warm but restrained
- interested but not overeager
- specific about fit
- brief

## Core positioning

Default positioning:
- forward-deployed AI engineer focused on hands-on implementation, integration, deployment, and debugging
- systems-oriented software engineer
- practical builder
- technically substantive problem solver
- building toward applied AI systems through customer workflow delivery

## Default response template

Thanks for reaching out. I'm most interested in forward-deployed and implementation-heavy AI roles where engineers own real integration, deployment, debugging, and customer workflow outcomes. My background spans practical software delivery, workflow and configuration systems, and hands-on ownership in real operating environments. I'd be glad to take a look if that sounds aligned.

## Variants by situation

### When the role looks promising

Express interest and ask for:
- job description
- compensation range
- location/remote expectations
- core technologies

### When the role is only partially aligned

Be polite and narrow the criteria.

### When the role is not aligned

Decline courteously without burning the bridge.

## Avoid

- oversharing
- emotional enthusiasm
- apologizing for boundaries
- talking about unemployment or runway
- talking about business plans
- lengthy autobiography
