# UI Compliance Notes

Canonical administrative notes as of 2026-03-31.

## Purpose

This document keeps unemployment-insurance and Michigan Works style process notes out of scattered issues and chats.

## General Rule

UI-related tasks should be handled as administrative maintenance:
- complete the requirement
- save confirmation details
- record any important deadline
- then reduce the task back down to a short issue or tracker note

## Michigan Works / Registration Guidance

The reusable process from the March 2026 notes is:

1. Create or update the profile on [Pure Michigan Talent Connect](https://www.mitalent.org/).
2. Upload or refresh the resume there if required.
3. Meet with a [Michigan Works!](https://www.michiganworks.org/) staff member, virtually or in person, so the profile can be verified.
4. Complete that verification before the relevant certification deadline rather than waiting until the certification date.
5. Keep any confirmation email or proof of verification.

## Michigan Works / Post Registration Compliance Guidance

## Practical Interpretation

- Coverage can begin before payment is released.
- Missing the registration / verification step can delay payment even if the claim itself exists.
- The safe approach is to finish the verification step at least one business day before certification.

## Recordkeeping

When a UI-related step matters, record:
- the requirement
- the date completed
- the confirmation received
- any next deadline

For job applications that may need to be defended in a UI audit, also record:
- the exact job posting URL
- the date applied
- the company and role title as submitted

The default place to keep the posting URL is the application's `job_description.md` file.

Short-lived execution should stay in GitHub issues or the project board. Durable guidance should stay in this doc.

## Scope Boundary

This repo is not meant to become a full personal-administration system. Only keep UI guidance here when it directly affects:
- job-search compliance
- certification timing
- application logging expectations
