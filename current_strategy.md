# Current Strategy

Canonical strategy as of 2026-06-16.

## Core Outcome

The current phase is a combined job-search, EA/UI compliance, employer-outreach, and market-research phase.

The goal is not simply to get any job quickly. The goal is to establish a truthful, market-legible identity while preserving runway and continuing to build evidence.

Applications during this phase are for:
- EA/UI compliance
- employer outreach
- market signal gathering
- identity testing
- interview calibration
- narrative refinement
- runway protection

Search-result deep dives are part of the market-research loop. After a search run, the useful question is not only "which jobs should Dorian apply to?" but also "what do these postings teach us about the resume, the proof gaps, and the most legible version of the Applied AI Systems Engineer identity?"

## Target Identity

Primary identity:

> Applied AI Systems Engineer

Working definition:

> A systems-oriented software engineer building applied AI systems around messy domain workflows, with emphasis on traceability, validation, deterministic boundaries, human review, and audit-ready evidence.

Specialty direction:
- document-heavy workflows
- compliance-sensitive systems
- legal, insurance, regulatory, and operational documents
- AI-assisted evidence pipelines
- RAG / retrieval / structured extraction
- workflow automation with human verification gates

This is an identity under construction. Do not present Dorian as a finished ML researcher, AI platform veteran, lawyer, compliance officer, or legal-domain authority. Present him as a serious software engineer moving into applied AI systems with unusually relevant evidence from legal-tech-debt, Areva compliance reporting, SED mission operations, RenoNerd business/software ownership, and emerging production-minded WindowConfigurator work.

## What Changed

The prior strategy was simulation/C++ first:

`systems C++ -> simulation -> real-time systems -> autonomy -> AI`

That remains a useful technical vector, but it is no longer the primary job-search identity.

The new strategy is:

`systems software + domain workflows + compliance/document experience -> applied AI systems -> document intelligence and evidence workflows -> production-minded AI engineering`

Simulation, C++, graphics, and real-time systems are preserved as depth signals and future optionality. They should not dominate employer-facing positioning unless the role itself is simulation/autonomy/technical R&D.

This project supports the claim that Dorian is building serious systems with real constraints. Do not overstate it as production-adjacent until the repo evidence supports that.

This project supports the claim that Dorian is building toward AI-assisted document intelligence and compliance evidence workflows. It is prototype/research evidence, not a production SaaS claim.

## Role Taxonomy

### A - Target

High-signal roles that directly build the new identity.

Examples:
- Applied AI Systems Engineer
- AI Solutions Engineer
- LLM / RAG Systems Engineer
- Document Intelligence Engineer
- AI Workflow / Automation Engineer
- Software Engineer, AI Systems
- AI Engineer for legal, insurance, compliance, or document-heavy workflows
- AI Governance / Controls Engineer, when implementation-heavy
- Forward Deployed AI Engineer, when the work includes real engineering in real workflows

### B - Strong Adjacent

Roles that do not say "AI" but strengthen the proof base.

Examples:
- C#/.NET Software Engineer on workflow, SaaS, integration, or platform systems
- Solutions Engineer / Implementation Engineer with real coding
- Compliance, insurance, legaltech, regtech, or document-workflow software engineer
- Internal tools engineer for document, reporting, or operations workflows
- Data/reporting engineer in compliance-sensitive environments
- Systems engineer or simulation engineer when the role reinforces rigor, traceability, or AI-adjacent autonomy/simulation work

### C - Compliance / Runway

Roles used to maintain EA/UI activity requirements or financial runway.

Rules:
- low customization
- low emotional investment
- fast logging in the tracker
- do not let them replace the identity-building work

Examples:
- broader .NET/web/software roles
- reporting/analytics roles
- business systems roles
- configuration/business-rules roles
- operations-oriented technical roles

## Guardrails

- Be honest about stage: transitioning into applied AI systems, not already a senior AI platform owner.
- Do not overclaim legal, compliance, actuarial, ML research, or production LLM platform authority.
- Do not undersell the engineering proof from WindowConfigurator.
- Do not let "AI" become vague branding; anchor it in documents, workflows, evidence, validation, and human review.
- Keep applications real and credible.
- Preserve learning time for AI systems, retrieval, future eval work, document processing, and production software boundaries.
- Keep C++/simulation as a secondary technical vector, not the main story unless the role demands it.
- Log every meaningful application.

## Energy and Fit Signals

Good signals:
- applied AI in real workflows
- document intelligence
- RAG / retrieval
- structured extraction
- workflow automation
- compliance, legal, insurance, or regulatory context
- evals and quality gates, as a future growth area unless current hands-on work exists
- traceability and audit evidence
- human-in-the-loop review
- systems architecture
- platform or integration boundaries
- technically serious teams

Avoid or de-prioritize:
- vague "AI enthusiast" roles with no engineering substance
- pure prompt-writing or content labeling roles
- pure ML research roles requiring credentials Dorian does not have
- compliance-policy roles with no software ownership
- legal roles requiring a JD or legal practice authority
- pure backend CRUD work with no AI, workflow, domain, or systems value
- high-volume sales or customer-success roles disguised as AI work

## Current Portfolio Anchor

The portfolio should be anchored by two complementary proofs:

1. WindowConfigurator: emerging production-minded, domain-constrained software engineering.
2. legal-tech-debt: applied AI/document intelligence research prototype with evidence pipelines and human-reviewable outputs.

The next useful proof should connect them:
- AI-assisted document/workflow system
- deterministic core with AI around the edges
- traceable evidence artifacts
- human review gates
- evals or regression checks for nondeterministic components, once hands-on work begins
