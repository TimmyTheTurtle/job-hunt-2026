## Workflow Findings

- The integration is working: the normal runner and ATS sweep produce meaningfully different candidate sets.

- The ATS sweep should stay in the job-search workflow, especially for Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, YC, and Hacker News.
- The runner scoring still needs some tuning around seniority and role mismatch. Staff/principal roles, pure SME roles, DataAnnotation-style AI trainer work, and wrong-country results should be demoted more strongly.
- No application decisions were recorded from this test. The run produced search ledger bookkeeping, but no applied/dismiss/shortlist decisions were intentionally written.
