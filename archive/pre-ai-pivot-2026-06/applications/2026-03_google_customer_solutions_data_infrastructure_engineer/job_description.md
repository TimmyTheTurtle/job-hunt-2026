About the job
As a Technical Solutions Consultant, you will be responsible for the technical relationship of our largest advertising clients or product partners. You will lead cross-functional teams in Engineering, Sales and Product Management to leverage emerging technologies for our external clients/partners. From concept design and testing to data analysis and support, you will oversee the technical execution and business operations of Google's online advertising platforms or product partnerships.

You will be able to balance business and partner needs with technical constraints, develop innovative, solutions and act as a partner and consultant to those you are working with. You will also be able to build tools and automate products, oversee the technical execution and business operations of Google's partnerships, as well as develop product strategy and prioritize projects and resources.

The Product and Sales Activation (PSA) team is responsible for performance and brand solutions for SMB advertisers around the world served by the Google Customer Solutions (GCS) business. We are the product and business experts behind the thousands of GCS Googlers that are responsible for driving sustainable business growth and product adoption. We identify opportunities through analysis, build consensus with stakeholders, and manage programs in cross-functional teams to drive GCS advertiser success and business growth.

In this role, you will build and maintain the core data infrastructure that supports our Sales and Partner teams operations and enables those teams to execute on our product objectives. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams, drive aspects of data infrastructure, and solution design and development (e.g., requirements gathering, testing, documentation, training, implementation, ongoing support, and maintenance).
Google Customer Solutions (GCS) sales teams are trusted advisors and competitive sellers who maintain a relentless focus on customer success by bringing the best Google has to offer to small- and medium-sized businesses (SMBs), which are the backbone of our communities. As a member of our team, you’ll have the opportunity to work with company owners and make a real difference in their businesses by helping them grow. Together, we help shape the future of innovation for customers, partners, and sellers...and we have fun doing it.
