## Role In Portfolio

The articles and personal site should explain the engineering judgment behind the projects:

- why agentic development needs phase boundaries,
- why context architecture matters,
- why evidence-first AI systems are different from chat wrappers,
- why nondeterministic systems need evals and human gates,
- and why legal/compliance/document workflows are a strong applied AI domain.

The site should route readers to working artifacts, not replace the artifacts.

If the site accepts inquiries, downloads, signups, or paid pilot requests, it should also have:

- basic inquiry workflow,
- and invoice or paid-pilot follow-up path.
